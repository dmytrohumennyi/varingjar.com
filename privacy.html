How Væringjar uses analytics and measurement technologies.

Væringjar is an independent research and teaching site. It shows no advertising.

Measurement

The site uses the Google tag to measure aggregate traffic and the effectiveness of campaigns that promote the site. Google may use cookies or similar technologies for this measurement. Where required, storage follows the visitor’s consent choice.

Language and theme preference

A local browser setting may be stored so the site remembers a manually selected EN, UA or DE language and the dark or light theme. These preferences are functional and are not used for profiling.

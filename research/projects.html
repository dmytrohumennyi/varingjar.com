Doctoral (Dr.Sc.) dissertation · topic approved by the department · 27.10.2026

Theoretical foundations for ensuring the survivability of supercritical real-time computer systems with dynamic architecture reconfiguration

Original title: «Теоретичні основи забезпечення живучості надкритичних комп’ютерних систем реального часу з динамічною реконфігурацією архітектури»

How can managed degradation, done by hand for decades, become a matter of calculation rather than expert judgement?

Aim. To solve the scientific and applied problem of keeping supercritical real-time computer systems in continuous operation by developing theoretical foundations for dynamic reconfiguration of their architecture, in which hardware and epistemic components are described by a single measure of operability, and the sequence of architectural changes is governed by admissibility criteria computable at design time.

Object. The reconfiguration process of a supercritical real-time computer system under degradation of its hardware and epistemic resources.

Subject. Quantitative admissibility criteria for reconfiguration — the measure of system capability in a degraded configuration, the trust threshold for the system’s own environment models and the lower bound on the interval between architectural changes — and the hardware and software means of implementing them in the structural and algorithmic organisation of such systems.

Specialty 05.13.05 — Computer systems and components · Doctoral candidate: Dmytro Humennyi, PhD, Associate Professor · Scientific consultant: not named here for personal-data reasons · Department of Software Engineering in Energy, Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute

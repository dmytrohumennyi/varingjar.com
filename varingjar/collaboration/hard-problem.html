Good collaboration input

A concrete hard problem is enough.

The useful starting point is not a polished proposal. Four pieces of information are enough to determine whether the problem overlaps with the research programme.

01
Plant

Robot, vehicle, flight platform or another cyber-physical object.

02
Damage / failure

What changes irreversibly or degrades during operation?

03
Residual resources

What sensing, actuation, compute, power or communication remains?

04
Residual objective

What useful behaviour should still be achieved and how can it be verified?

ACADEMIC PARTICIPATION // Selected research tasks may be developed through PhD and MSc work supervised by Dmytro Humennyi. Academic participation does not change the ownership or funding model of Væringjar II.
Open dialogue

If the problem overlaps, start a technical conversation.

No procurement process or commercial scope is required. A research question, paper, plant model, test scenario or implementation idea is enough to start.

Control-execution hierarchy

Different layers still run at different rates.

The abstraction hierarchy says what the system means. The execution hierarchy says where decisions are computed and how quickly they act.

  1. L3 Mission Manager — trajectory objective, priorities and horizon.
  2. L2 Supervisor — diagnosis, validity, graph planning and reconfiguration.
  3. L1 Servo Control — plant regulation inside the active configuration.
  4. L0 Hardware — sensing, actuation, interrupts and physical I/O.
Timing contract

The architecture must satisfy three independent temporal constraints.

SchedulerEach task meets its deadline
\(R_i\le D_i\)
ReactionFault-to-switch latency fits the mode budget
\(\theta+R_{dec}+t_{switch}\le t_{max}\)
EvolutionSwitches are not too frequent
\(t_{k+1}-t_k\ge\tau_d\)

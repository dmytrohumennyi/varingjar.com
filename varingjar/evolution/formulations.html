Turning point

EXP‑03 answered its question — and made the next question unavoidable.

WHAT EXP‑03 ESTABLISHED

Structural reconfiguration can preserve a degraded mission.

  • irreversible resource loss is represented explicitly;
  • candidate configurations are constrained by a directed graph;
  • mission functionality and survivability can drive the choice;
  • the executed path is auditable and reproducible.
WHAT IT DID NOT ESTABLISH

The selected sequence is not yet a guaranteed trajectory.

  • \(\gamma(c)<1\) verifies each configuration, not arbitrary repeated switching;
  • \(\arg\max S\) chooses the best node now, not the best path over a dwell interval;
  • hardware operability alone cannot represent a model that is healthy in memory but wrong about the world;
  • reaction time says how fast to switch, not how frequently switching remains admissible.
